Projects

Selected projects

Research tools, experiments, and working systems.

AI SAFETY POLIS↗ An experimental platform for designing and testing institutions for multi-agent AI systems. AI EVALUATION SafeguardShift↗ Measuring agent behavior when verification, review, and other operational safeguards are lost. ML RELIABILITY PipelineProof↗ Execution-based verification of silent integrity failures in machine-learning pipelines. EVALUATION SCIENCE Probe Scent↗ Testing how readily AI models recognize scenarios that resemble evaluations. MACHINE UNLEARNING Unlearning at Scale↗ Trace-preserving training replay for reproducible, state-exact data deletion. INTERPRETABILITY Markovian Circuit Tracing↗ Testing transformer state abstractions against known dynamics and causal ground truth. SOCIAL SIMULATION LLM Persona↗ Studying when better synthetic individuals fail to produce more faithful populations. ALGORITHMIC FAIRNESS Status Association Audit↗ Separating socioeconomic associations from their effects on consequential AI decisions. TELECOMMUNICATIONS ICR-OPT↗ Simulating mobile-network sharing, capacity constraints, and cooperative settlements. SIGNAL PROCESSING OpenVQ↗ A speech-quality research engine with interpretable signal and telecom diagnostics. OPERATIONS RESEARCH Queue Topology Lab↗ Interactive simulation and optimization of finite-capacity service networks. COMPUTATIONAL SYSTEMS Path-Dependent Radix Spaces↗ Exact ranking, enumeration, and deterministic sampling of structured finite domains. FINANCIAL COMPUTING FinSpace↗ Rank-addressable financial scenarios for reproducible testing and simulation. PUBLIC GOVERNANCE Public Sector AI Survey↗ A paired-choice study of public preferences for oversight of AI-assisted decisions. EDUCATION DATA UDISE+ Exposure Study↗ Reproducible data pipelines for school access, composition, and infrastructure research. ECONOMIC RESEARCH SEC Cyber Disclosure Timing↗ Analyzing cybersecurity disclosure timing and financial-market responses.
