A little about me.

At a glance

I study how advanced AI systems behave, and how the institutions around them can make them safer.

I'm the founder and president of Project AWARE, an international think tank working on institutional AI safety through research, diplomacy and capacity building.

My work moves between technical safety research, public policy and institution-building, with a particular interest in how governments can govern frontier AI in practice.
